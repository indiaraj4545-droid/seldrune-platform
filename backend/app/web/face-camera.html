CONNECT TO YOUR CALL

Three steps to go live

  1. Connect your live profile

    Use the authenticated session studio below. Keep the visible AI-modified indicator enabled.

  2. Start OBS Virtual Camera

    Open clean output. In OBS, add a Window Capture of that window, fit it to the canvas, then start Virtual Camera. Capture the output window only.

  3. Select your meeting camera

    Choose OBS Virtual Camera in the meeting app. Keep your usual microphone selected.
